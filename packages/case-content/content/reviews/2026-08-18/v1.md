# Whereabouts themed review: 2026-08-18

## Theme: Pre-1900 Botanical Gardens in Cities

Step inside the living green collections that have shaped city life for more than a century.

### Inclusion criteria

Include a location only when all conditions are met: (1) it is an individually named institution whose official name or governing authority identifies it as a botanical garden, botanic garden, or equivalent historical botanical-garden institution; (2) the garden was established or opened at the same identifiable site on or before 31 December 1899; (3) the historic garden still physically exists as a maintained garden; (4) it is regularly open to ordinary visitors, whether free or ticketed; (5) the garden lies within the current administrative boundary of a present-day city or incorporated urban municipality; and (6) the exact garden can be pinned as a single visitable location rather than an entire metropolitan region.
