# Whereabouts themed review: 2026-09-01

## Theme: Historic Working City Halls

Stone towers, clocklit chambers, and civic squares still anchor the daily life of cities around the world.

### Inclusion criteria

Include a location only when every condition is met: (1) it is an individually named city hall, town hall, municipal hall, Rathaus, hôtel de ville, ayuntamiento, palazzo comunale, or clearly equivalent building; (2) the present building, or a continuously recognized predecessor occupying the same identifiable site, was completed, opened for civic use, or first established there on or before 31 December 1899; (3) the building still houses the government of its present-day city or incorporated municipality, including the mayor’s office, council chamber, municipal administration, or another official core civic function, on the date of play; (4) it occupies a fixed, individually mappable building or contiguous civic complex with a recognizable exterior or public entrance; (5) the site lies within the current administrative boundary of a present-day city or incorporated urban municipality; and (6) the location is identifiable by its official civic name rather than only by a surrounding square, district, palace, or administrative campus. The worldwide set should provide at least 35 plausible locations across many cities and countries.
