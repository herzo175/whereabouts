# Whereabouts themed review: 2026-08-25

## Theme: Operating Opera Houses Opened Before 1900

Velvet, gilded balconies, and familiar overtures turn historic city centers into stages for a daily journey through living opera.

### Inclusion criteria

Include a location only when every condition is met: (1) it is an individually named opera house, opera theatre, or equivalent venue whose principal identity is as a public opera-performing institution; (2) the venue first opened to the public at its current identifiable site on or before 31 December 1899; (3) the present venue remains physically extant at that site, even if it was later reconstructed, restored, or substantially remodeled; (4) it is operating on the date of play as a public performing-arts venue and regularly presents fully staged opera productions under its own programme or resident-company programme; (5) performances are available to ordinary members of the public, whether ticketed or occasionally free; (6) the venue lies within the current administrative boundary of a present-day city or incorporated urban municipality; and (7) the opera house, its principal building, or its public entrance can be pinned as one exact map location. This definition is intended to yield a corpus of at least 35 plausible locations across multiple cities and countries.
