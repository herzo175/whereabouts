# Whereabouts themed review: 2026-08-25

## Theme: Operating Historic Covered Markets

Under iron, glass, and weathered stone, city markets keep their old rhythms: a daily hunt for food, craft, and human noise.

### Inclusion criteria

Include a location only when every condition is met: (1) it is an individually named permanent public market, market hall, covered market, or roofed bazaar; (2) its principal function is selling food, household goods, crafts, or other merchandise from public-facing stalls; (3) substantial permanent market structures at the current identifiable site opened for market trade on or before 31 December 1899; (4) the market still operates at that site on the date of play and is regularly accessible to ordinary visitors, whether free or ticketed; (5) it lies within the current administrative boundary of a present-day city or incorporated urban municipality; and (6) the named market, market building, or principal public entrance can be pinned as one exact map location.
