# Whereabouts themed review: 2026-08-25

## Theme: Operating Passenger Railway Stations Opened Before 1900

Before air travel, grand stations were gateways to the wider world. These historic terminals still carry passengers today.

### Inclusion criteria

Include only a location satisfying every condition: (1) it is an individually named railway station with platforms or a station building that can be pinned as one exact map location; (2) scheduled passenger rail service began at that same identifiable site on or before 31 December 1899, even if the station building was later rebuilt or replaced; (3) the station currently has regularly scheduled passenger trains on the date of play, including intercity, regional, commuter, or national rail; (4) the station lies within the current administrative boundary of a present-day city or incorporated urban municipality; and (5) the qualifying historical opening date and present passenger operation are documented or generally unambiguous.
