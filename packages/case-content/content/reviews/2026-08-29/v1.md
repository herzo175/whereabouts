# Whereabouts themed review: 2026-08-29

## Theme: Historic Public Libraries in Cities

Quiet halls gather the memory of whole cities: grand reading rooms, worn steps, and shelves that have welcomed generations of curious visitors.

### Inclusion criteria

Include a location only when every condition is met: (1) it is an individually named, permanent public-serving library institution, such as a city, municipal, national, state, or provincial library; (2) the institution was founded, chartered, or opened for library service on or before 31 December 1899; (3) it still operates as a library at a fixed site on the date of play; (4) ordinary visitors can enter a public service area, reading room, reference room, or equivalent during published or customary hours, subject only to routine registration, identification, or bag-check requirements; (5) the site lies within the current administrative boundary of a present-day city or incorporated urban municipality; (6) the principal library facility or independently named library occupies a single mappable location with a recognizable public entrance; and (7) its principal function remains providing public access to books, periodicals, or other library collections, whether by lending, reference use, or supervised reading.
