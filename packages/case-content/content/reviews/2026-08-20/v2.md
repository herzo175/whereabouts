# Whereabouts themed review: 2026-08-20

## Theme: Public Aquariums in Cities

Ocean life comes ashore in cities around the world. These public aquariums turn marine discovery into an urban experience.

### Inclusion criteria

Include a location only when every condition is met: (1) it is an individually named, permanent public aquarium, oceanarium, or aquarium complex whose principal visitor function is displaying living aquatic animals or aquatic ecosystems; (2) it has regularly scheduled public admission on the date of play, whether ticketed or free; (3) the named aquarium occupies a specific permanent site that can be pinned as one exact map location; (4) that site lies within the current administrative boundary of a present-day city or incorporated urban municipality; and (5) the institution is operating under its own identifiable name, rather than being merely an unnamed exhibit within another attraction.
