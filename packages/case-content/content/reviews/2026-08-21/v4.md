# Whereabouts themed review: 2026-08-21

## Theme: Operating Aerial Passenger Cableways in Cities

Above busy streets, cable cars trace unlikely paths through the sky. These urban aerial routes still carry passengers today.

### Inclusion criteria

Include a location only when every condition is met: (1) it is an individually named aerial passenger cableway, gondola lift, aerial tramway, cable car, teleférico, téléphérique, telecabine, or equivalent service; (2) passenger cabins, cars, or gondolas travel suspended above the ground on an overhead cable system rather than on rails; (3) it provides regularly scheduled public passenger service on the date of play, whether ordinary urban transit or a regularly open visitor attraction; (4) at least one named terminal or station lies within the current administrative boundary of a present-day city or incorporated urban municipality; and (5) the cableway, line, or a specific station can be pinned as one exact, visitable map location.
