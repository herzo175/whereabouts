# Whereabouts themed review: 2026-08-27

## Theme: Operating Pre-1900 Railway Stations in Cities

Steel, glass, and old platforms gather the world’s city stories. Find stations where yesterday’s railways still welcome today’s travelers.

### Inclusion criteria

Include a location only when every condition is met: (1) it is an individually named heavy-rail passenger station or terminal; (2) the station or its immediately identifiable predecessor began railway passenger service at the same site on or before 31 December 1899; (3) regularly scheduled public passenger trains currently serve that station on the date of play; (4) the station occupies a fixed, mappable site with a recognizable public entrance or concourse; (5) it lies within the current administrative boundary of a present-day city or incorporated urban municipality; and (6) the exact station, rather than an entire rail district or line, can be used as the game location. Plausible locations include St Pancras International, King’s Cross, London Paddington, London Waterloo, Liverpool Lime Street, Manchester Victoria, Glasgow Central, Edinburgh Waverley, York railway station, Bristol Temple Meads, Brighton railway station, Gare du Nord, Gare de l’Est, Gare Saint-Lazare, Gare d’Austerlitz, Amsterdam Centraal, Zürich Hauptbahnhof, Bern railway station, Stockholm Central Station, Kraków Główny, Wrocław Główny, Prague Main Railway Station, Budapest Keleti, Budapest Nyugati, Ljubljana railway station, Bratislava hlavná stanica, București Nord, Sofia Central Station, Roma Termini, Wien Praterstern, Trieste Centrale, Leningradsky railway station, Sirkeci railway station, Ramses Station, Alexandria Misr railway station, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus, Sealdah railway station, Chennai Central, Rio de Janeiro Central do Brasil, Buenos Aires Constitución, and Buenos Aires Once.
