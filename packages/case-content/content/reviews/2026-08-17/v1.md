# Whereabouts themed review: 2026-08-17

## Theme: UNESCO World Heritage Sites Inside National Capitals

History hides in plain sight across the world's capitals. These landmarks carry stories recognized far beyond their city streets.

### Inclusion criteria

Include only a location that satisfies all of these conditions: (1) it is an individually named UNESCO World Heritage property or an individually named component of a UNESCO World Heritage property; (2) the property or component is physically located within the current municipal boundaries of a present-day national capital city; (3) its UNESCO World Heritage inscription is active, not merely proposed or tentative; and (4) the exact site can be identified on a map as a visitable landmark, complex, district, building, garden, or archaeological area. The country’s constitutional, executive, or de facto capital may qualify only if it is the officially recognized current national capital at the time of play.
