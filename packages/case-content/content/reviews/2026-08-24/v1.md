# Whereabouts themed review: 2026-08-24

## Theme: Operating Public Observation Decks in Landmark High-Rises

Cities reveal a different character from high above. These public observation decks turn skylines into destinations.

### Inclusion criteria

Include a location only when every condition is met: (1) it is an individually named, permanent observation deck, observation level, tower observatory, skydeck, or equivalent public viewing venue; (2) the venue is physically located within a high-rise building, freestanding tower, or purpose-built landmark observation structure, with its principal attraction being a panoramic view from an elevated position; (3) it offers regularly scheduled public admission on the date of play, whether ticketed or free, rather than access only through a private event or hotel booking; (4) the named deck or observatory has a fixed, mappable entrance or visitor facility that can be treated as one exact location; (5) the structure lies within the current administrative boundary of a present-day city or incorporated urban municipality; and (6) the venue is identifiable by its own public-facing name or by the name of the tower or high-rise containing it.
