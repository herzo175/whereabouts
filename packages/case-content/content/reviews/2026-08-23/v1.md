### Inclusion criteria

Include a location only when every condition is met: (1) it is an individually named, permanent planetarium venue, planetarium theater, or equivalent facility whose principal public function is presenting astronomical or space-science shows in a dome or other purpose-built immersive theater; (2) it has regularly scheduled public sessions or shows and ordinary public admission, whether ticketed or free, on the date of play; (3) the named venue is physically operating at a fixed site rather than being mobile or temporary; (4) the site lies within the current administrative boundary of a present-day city or incorporated urban municipality; and (5) the exact planetarium venue or its entrance can be pinned as one visitable map location. A planetarium located inside a museum or science center qualifies if the planetarium itself has an identifiable name and public program.
