# Whereabouts themed review: 2026-08-28

## Theme: Operating Pre-1900 Theatres in Cities

Across old city streets, stage doors still open onto velvet, brass, and living echoes of centuries of performance.

### Inclusion criteria

Include a location only when every condition is met: (1) it is an individually named theatre, opera house, playhouse, or equivalent venue whose principal function is presenting live theatrical, operatic, ballet, or musical performances; (2) the named venue or its continuously recognized predecessor began public performance at the same identifiable site on or before 31 December 1899; (3) the venue currently operates as a permanent performance venue and has regularly scheduled public performances on the date of play; (4) the venue occupies a fixed, mappable building or complex with a public entrance that can be treated as one exact location; (5) it lies within the current administrative boundary of a present-day city or incorporated urban municipality; and (6) ordinary members of the public can attend performances, whether ticketed or free. Rebuilt venues qualify when the theatre institution or venue remains at the same identifiable site. The theme has at least 35 plausible locations, including: Royal Opera House (London); Theatre Royal Drury Lane (London); Theatre Royal Haymarket (London); Bristol Old Vic (Bristol); Palais Garnier (Paris); Comédie-Française, Salle Richelieu (Paris); Théâtre du Châtelet (Paris); Vienna State Opera (Vienna); Hungarian State Opera (Budapest); Estates Theatre (Prague); National Theatre (Prague); Bolshoi Theatre (Moscow); Mariinsky Theatre (Saint Petersburg); Odesa National Academic Opera and Ballet Theatre (Odesa); Teatro alla Scala (Milan); Teatro di San Carlo (Naples); Teatro Argentina (Rome); Teatro Massimo (Palermo); Teatro Olimpico (Vicenza); Teatro Comunale di Bologna (Bologna); Teatro Español (Madrid); Teatro de la Zarzuela (Madrid); Teatro Real (Madrid); Gran Teatre del Liceu (Barcelona); Teatro Nacional de São Carlos (Lisbon); Teatro Nacional D. Maria II (Lisbon); Manoel Theatre (Valletta); Teatro Amazonas (Manaus); Teatro Nacional Sucre (Quito); Teatro de Cristóbal Colón (Bogotá); Teatro Municipal de Santiago (Santiago); Teatro Solís (Montevideo); Teatro Degollado (Guadalajara); Teatro Morelos (Aguascalientes); Teatro de la Paz (San Luis Potosí); Teatro Calderón (Valladolid); Teatro Arriaga (Bilbao); Teatro Romea (Murcia); Teatro Principal (Zaragoza); Teatro Municipal de Valparaíso (Valparaíso); Tbilisi Opera and Ballet Theatre (Tbilisi); Teatro de la República (Querétaro); Grand Theatre of Warsaw (Warsaw); Teatro Regio (Turin).
