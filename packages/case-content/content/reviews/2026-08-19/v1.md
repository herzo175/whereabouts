# Whereabouts themed review: 2026-08-19

## Theme: Operating Funicular Railways in Cities

Some city streets are simply too steep for ordinary rail. These enduring funiculars have climbed their hills for generations.

### Inclusion criteria

Include a location only when all conditions are met: (1) it is an individually named railway, funicular, ascensor, standseilbahn, cable railway, or equivalent officially recognized funicular service; (2) vehicles travel on fixed rails up a sustained incline and are hauled or counterbalanced by a cable, including accepted single-track or single-car funicular designs; (3) it provides regularly scheduled public passenger service on the date of play, whether operated as transit or as a regularly open visitor railway; (4) at least one station or terminal lies within the current administrative boundary of a present-day city or incorporated urban municipality; and (5) the railway or a specific station can be pinned as one exact, visitable map location.
