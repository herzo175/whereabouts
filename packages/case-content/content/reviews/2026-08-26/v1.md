# Whereabouts themed review: 2026-08-26

## Theme: Operating Historic Covered Markets in Cities

Commerce has gathered beneath these roofs for generations. These historic covered markets remain lively centers of city life.

### Inclusion criteria

Include a location only when every condition is met: (1) it is an individually named permanent market hall, covered market, covered bazaar, or covered market street whose principal function is public retail trading in food, household goods, crafts, or other ordinary merchandise; (2) it has regularly operating public stalls or shops on the date of play and is ordinarily accessible to visitors during published or customary market hours; (3) the market occupies a fixed, mappable complex, building, or contiguous covered street that can be treated as one exact location; (4) the market was established, opened, or first documented at that same identifiable site on or before 31 December 1899, even if its roof or buildings were later rebuilt; (5) it lies within the current administrative boundary of a present-day city or incorporated urban municipality; and (6) its market identity and historical date are documented or widely unambiguous from official or reputable sources.
